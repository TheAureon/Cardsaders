How to play

  • WASD move · mouse aim · left click or Space punch · Q/E or right-drag turn camera · Esc pause
  • Each round you get 7 random cards. Double-click a card (or press 1-7) to play it. Unplayed cards are lost when the round ends.
  • Punches do 1 damage. +N cards add damage, ×2 multiplies the whole punch. Every buff lasts 30 sec and stacks.
  • Block stops everything for 3 sec. Reverse sends everything back for 3 sec.
  • Skip and Discard cards hit whoever you punched last. The target gets a 1 sec warning to Block or Reverse.
  • Discards also let you spy: you see the cards they remove. Plain Discard lets you pick any card from their hand.
  • HP carries over between rounds. Last fighter standing wins.
